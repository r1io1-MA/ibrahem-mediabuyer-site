<p>You do not have to be an ads expert to know whether your account is run well. You only need to ask the right questions. I ask these seven on every new account I take over, and you can ask them of whoever runs your ads today.</p>
<ol class="steps">
<li><b>Do the platform numbers match store orders?</b> Ask for a simple comparison: purchases in the ad platforms against orders in Salla, Zid or Shopify. A small gap is normal. A large gap means tracking needs fixing and decisions are being made on wrong data.</li>
<li><b>What was MER this month?</b> Total store revenue ÷ total ad spend. If whoever runs your account does not track this, they are only tracking what the platforms say, and every platform credits itself with more than it deserves.</li>
<li><b>How much of Google's results come from people searching your brand name?</b> Brand campaigns show huge returns because people searching your name would usually buy anyway. In <a href="/cases/edtech-251-percent-of-target/">one case</a> the reported return was 10.5x; with brand removed it was 3.2x. Ask to see the two numbers separately.</li>
<li><b>Do the audiences overlap?</b> If several ad sets target the same people, they bid against each other and raise your costs. Ask: are past buyers excluded? Are retargeting audiences kept separate from prospecting?</li>
<li><b>How many new ads were tested this month?</b> Creative is the biggest driver of performance today. An account that does not test new ads regularly will decline over time, however good its settings are.</li>
<li><b>What is the cost-per-order ceiling, and where did it come from?</b> There should be a clear number calculated from your margin, not a generic benchmark. You can work it out yourself with the <a href="/tools/break-even-roas/">break-even ROAS calculator</a>, and check real profit with the <a href="/tools/roi-calculator/">ROI calculator</a>.</li>
<li><b>Does the report talk about your sales, or about impressions and clicks?</b> A good report starts with revenue, orders and cost per order against target, then explains what will change next week and why.</li>
</ol>
<h2>If the answers are unclear</h2>
<p>It does not necessarily mean the person running your account is bad, but it does mean money is being spent without the full picture. Start with the first two questions; everything else is built on them.</p>
