Reading Google honestly

10.5xBlended account ROAS, inflated by brand searches that would have converted anyway.
3.2xNon-brand ROAS with brand protection removed. Budgets were set on this number.

74% of reported value came from one brand-protection campaign on 16% of spend. I reported it separately so management could see what Google actually acquired, and ran competitor search (2.2x) on a capped budget.

What I did

  • Split the month into two jobs. The first half sold a "catch-up" angle to parents after the first school tests. The second half used National Day for reach, then converted that audience with retargeting.
  • Tested 33 creators as ad inventory. One creator per ad set beside a standard-creative ad set, on ad-set budgets, so winners showed within days and losers were cut early.
  • Cleaned the audience map. Prospecting, lookalike and retargeting audiences that don't overlap, with purchasers excluded, so ad sets stopped bidding against each other.
  • Fixed attribution first. UTM gaps had pushed half of all revenue into "Direct". I rebuilt UTMs for every channel and creator link before raising spend.
  • Automated the lead side. Snapchat lead forms feed a shared sheet and alert the call-center team in real time, with ads scheduled to call-center hours.
